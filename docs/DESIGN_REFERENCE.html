# Product Requirements Document: Landmark Operations & Staffing App ## 1. Project Overview A specialized application designed for a National Landmark operating 7 days a week, integrated with the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America (GOA) calendar. The app manages staffing, scheduling, clock-ins, and operational communication for staff, managers, and directors. ## 2. Core Operational Logic ### Operating Hours * **Site Open:** 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM * **Public Hours:** 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM * **Service Days:** Guided by the GOA Archdiocese calendar. ### Staffing Rules * **Daily Base (Mon-Sat):** 1 Greeter, 2 Operations Personnel,1 Security Guards. * **Director of Operations:** Assigned to all major church services/holidays. * **After-Hours Events (Post 5 PM):** Minimum 1 Operations + 1 Security Guard. ### Shift & Break Management * **Shift Length:** 7.5 hours total. * **Break Schedule:** * 2.5 hours in: 15-minute break. * 5.5 hours in (3 hours after 1st): 30-minute break. * 8.5 hours in? *(Note: User specified 15 min after another 3 hours before clock out at 7.5 hrs. Logic needs alignment: 2.5h + 15m + 3h + 30m + 1.75h = 7.5h shift total)*. * **Clock-in:** Geofenced to site location. * **Alerts:** Push notification 30 minutes before scheduled start. ## 3. Key Functional Features * **Calendar Integration:** Sync with GOA Archdiocese calendar (names, descriptions, services). * **Digital Chant Stand Links:** Direct access to service summaries and liturgical texts. * **Backend Analysis:** * Overtime reduction logic. * Scheduling optimization based on visitor volume. * **Communication:** P2P texting/messaging within the app. * **Media Upload:** Staff folders for text, video, and image submissions. ## 4. Proposed Screen Map 1. **Landing & Auth:** Splash screen, Sign Up/In with role selection (Staff vs. Manager). 2. **Staff Dashboard (The "Show"):** Current shift status, countdown to next break, geofenced clock-in button, and today's service summary from the Digital Chant Stand. 3. **Operations Calendar:** Monthly/Weekly view integrating GOA liturgical dates with staffing assignments. 4. **Manager's Command Center:** Scheduling analysis, visitor-to-staff ratios, overtime alerts, and staff folder management. 5. **P2P Messaging:** Internal chat interface for staff coordination. 6. **Profile & Media:** Personal schedule history and file upload area. ## 5. Technical Recommendations * **Frontend:** React Native. * **Backend:** Node.js/Python with a robust relational database (PostgreSQL) for complex scheduling logic. * **Integrations:** Google Calendar API, GOA Digital Chant Stand RSS/API, Google Maps/CoreLocation for geofencing.
