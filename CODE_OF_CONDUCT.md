# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.
