The operational source of truth for the National Shrine.

Plain-English Breakdown

One place for every operational moving part.

Instead of scattered spreadsheets, paper sign-in sheets, radio calls, text threads, and disconnected files, Shrine Ops gives the team one trusted place to see what is happening, what needs attention, who is assigned, and what has already been completed.

Payroll & Reporting

Cleaner payroll preparation and better monthly reporting.

Shrine Ops helps turn daily staff activity into organized reports. Hours, shifts, overtime flags, tickets, walkthroughs, incidents, and visitor trends can be reviewed from one place, helping leadership see both the daily picture and the monthly story.

1

Staff clock in and out

The system records shift activity and keeps an accurate history for review.

2

Hours are organized by pay period

Managers can review regular hours, overtime concerns, and gross pay summaries.

3

Reports become easier to produce

Monthly summaries help show staffing, maintenance activity, incidents, and overall operational workload.

Employee Recognition

Built-in morale, pride, and achievement.

The recognition program celebrates the people who keep the Shrine ready, clean, safe, and welcoming. Staff earn points and badges for positive contributions such as completing walkthroughs, resolving tickets, showing up on time, helping with events, and going above and beyond.

Top contributors can be highlighted formally, reinforcing pride and long-term engagement.
